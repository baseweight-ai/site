Talk to Philip Stevens

The fastest path is booking a diagnostic call: 30 minutes to walk through your failure patterns, current setup, and what the right intervention looks like.

You’ll leave the call with a clear view of what’s failing and why, whether the diagnostic is the right next step, and what fixing it would require. No upsell. No vague roadmap. A concrete, actionable assessment, whether or not we work together.

Typical questions the intro call covers:

  • Is your retrieval bottleneck a chunking problem or an embedding problem?
  • Does your structured extraction failure mode indicate a fine-tune candidate or a prompt engineering gap?
  • Is your inference latency a model size problem or a serving infrastructure problem?
  • Are you losing accuracy at inference time because of distribution shift or inadequate training signal?

If email is a better fit, use the address below. Response is typically within one business day. For project inquiries, include your current setup and the core problem in a paragraph.

We work with teams past the prompt-tuning ceiling. If you haven’t shipped to production yet, come back when you have failures to diagnose.
