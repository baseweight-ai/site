Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026.

What we collect

Booking a call goes through Cal.com, which collects your name, email address, timezone and any notes you add, and passes them to us so we can meet. No other form on this site collects data. If you email us, we hold your address and your message.

We also use Vercel's analytics to count page views. These are measured without cookies and are not linked to your email address.

How we use it

  • To reply to you if you write to us.

We will never use your email address for any purpose beyond the above. We do not add you to unrelated mailing lists, and we do not send promotional material on behalf of third parties.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, retargeting, or profiling.
  • We do not use cookies to track you.

Third-party services

Email to us is handled by Google's mail service. This site is hosted by Vercel, which also provides the analytics described above. Call bookings are handled by Cal.com. The privacy policies of Google, Vercel and Cal.com apply to the data they process.

Data retention

We keep what you submit until you ask us to delete it. On request we delete your email address within 30 days.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to withdraw consent for its use at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@baseweight.co.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. We will not retroactively reduce your rights without notifying you directly.
