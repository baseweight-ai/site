Why benchmark first

Don’t commit to a build you haven’t proven.

Plenty of vendors will happily train you a model. What they hand back is a loss curve and a bill — not proof the result is actually better on your task than what you have now. By the time you can tell, the money’s already spent.

The Pilot inverts that. For $3–5k, fixed scope, we run a custom open model head-to-head against your current option on a sample of your real data and give you a go/no-go verdict with the reason. You make the build decision with evidence, not a vendor’s default.

And the eval harness behind the verdict is yours to keep, whatever you decide next. Build with us, take it to another team, or run it in-house — you own the thing that proves the win.

The expensive mistake isn’t the build. It’s the build you couldn’t prove was better.
