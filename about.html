About Baseweight

Every AI shop has a tool they’re trying to sell you. We don’t have one.

Every AI services company has a specialty, and it’s usually the thing they sell. Fine-tuning shops recommend fine-tuning. RAG consultancies recommend RAG. The recommendation follows from their default, not from what actually wins on your task.

Baseweight starts somewhere else: prove it first. Before anyone commits to a build, we benchmark a custom open model you’d own against your current option — on your data, with an eval calibrated to your task. The technique follows from what the benchmark shows, not from a house style.

That matters most when a frontier API isn’t the answer — the data can’t leave your walls, the per-call bill is brutal at your scale, or the general model is simply wrong too often on your task. The deliverable is intelligence you own and the proof it works, not a dependency on us.

Every engagement produces artifacts you own outright: weights or adapters, training recipes, the eval harness, inference code. You can run it, extend it, or take it elsewhere. Retainers exist, but the goal is never lock-in.

What We Believe

Proof before build

We benchmark whether an owned model wins on your task before anyone commits to building it. The evidence comes first.

Ownership over dependency

You should own your model, your weights, and the eval that proves it works. Full stop.

Patterns over bespoke

Each engagement extends a library of task and failure patterns built across clients and verticals. The second time we see a pattern, the work is faster and better grounded. We don’t start from zero.

Honesty over revenue

If an owned model won’t beat what you have, we’ll tell you — and you keep the eval that says so. We’d rather earn trust than manufacture scope.
