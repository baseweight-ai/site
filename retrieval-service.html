Retrieval service

What this is

A retrieval-augmented question-answering service over English Wikipedia. It retrieves passages, answers from them, and cites the passages it used. An eval harness in its repository scores each change against a fixed test set.

Demo

The index holds a capped subset of English Wikipedia, frozen at one past dump. Anything newer, this week’s news included, is outside it.

One question is an anecdote. The results below measure the service over its test set.

How it’s measured

Retrieval is scored against the source pages the dataset records for each question, so its ground truth comes from the data. Answers are scored by a language model acting as judge.

  • Gated: retrieval scores that block a merge when a change regresses them.
  • Advisory: scores that are reported and block nothing. The judged scores are advisory: they cover a small hand-written set, where the confidence interval is wide enough that a gate would fire on noise.

Confidence intervals come from a paired bootstrap clustered by source page, because several questions can share one source page.
