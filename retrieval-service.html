    How it’s measured, and its limits

    Retrieval is scored against the source pages the dataset records for each question, so its ground truth comes from the data. Answers are scored by a language model acting as judge.

    Gated
    A change that regresses the score cannot merge.
    Advisory
    A change that regresses the score can still merge.

    The judged scores are advisory: they cover a small hand-written set, where the confidence interval is wide enough that a gate would fire on noise.

    Confidence intervals come from a paired bootstrap clustered by source page, because several questions can share one source page.

    Each score shows n, the number of questions behind it, and a CI, the range the true score likely sits in.
